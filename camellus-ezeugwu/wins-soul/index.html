Core terms

  • Grace. Grace is a free gift, given by God for the benefit of man.
  • Free Will. Free Will is God’s first Grace gift to man, a delegated authority to govern within an assigned domain.
  • Free Choice. The Second Grace (Free Choice) is the authorized exercise of Free Will within divine boundaries.
  • Alignment. Alignment is the state in which man’s delegated will agrees with God’s sovereign will, through authorized choice, in response to Grace. This is what God tests.
  • Redemption. Jesus redeemed Free Will and Free Choice.
  • Salvation. Eternal salvation is only achieved by believing in Jesus.
  • Righteousness. Righteousness is God’s declaration of approval over aligned Faith, not a reward for spiritual performance.
