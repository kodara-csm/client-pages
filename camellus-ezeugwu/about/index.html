About WINS

WINS is a durable, multilingual global platform that combines trusted knowledge, artificial intelligence, and human networks to prevent disease of the Heart and Soul.

WINS is an education and prevention platform. It does not diagnose, prescribe, change medications, or give individualized treatment. Its primary purpose is nonprofit public education and Concept Book teaching.
