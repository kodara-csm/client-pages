FAQ

Questions, answered.

If this were real, wouldn't my doctor have told me?

The research is ahead of standard practice. Peer-reviewed work has identified genetic associations with traits related to self-regulation and addiction, but most clinical settings still prescribe by symptom and adjust by trial and error. The science is there. The application is still catching up.

Do I have to be in crisis for this to matter?

No. If you're using something to manage your stress, energy, anxiety or sleep and you can feel things heading the wrong way, that's exactly when understanding your biology makes the biggest difference. The assessment doesn't ask you to call yourself anything.

Can I take the assessment for someone I love?

Yes. Many of the people Dr. Higgins works with were first found by a spouse or a parent. You can go through it thinking about their situation and bring them real information instead of another argument.

Is the assessment a diagnosis?

No. It's education, not diagnosis or treatment, and it doesn't replace testing or a clinical conversation. It gives you an honest, personalized read on what is likely going on and what a biology-first path could look like.
