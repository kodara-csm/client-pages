About Dr. Evelyn Higgins

The doctor for people whose biology was missed.

Dr. Evelyn Higgins, DC, CAd, DACACD, DABDA, is a globally recognized expert in health, neuroscience and personalized medicine, with nearly 40 years in clinical practice and consulting.

Her story

Health is more than simply the absence of disease.

For most of her career, Dr. Higgins has worked with people who had already tried everything. Years on the same medication. Therapy that helped for a while. Facilities that cost a fortune and changed nothing once they got home. Again and again, once she looked at their biology, she found something nobody had looked at before.

What she does now

She founded Wired BioHealth™ and Wired For Addiction™, two systems that combine lab-based neuroscience and epigenetics to address mood, stress, burnout, sleep, cravings and the patterns underneath them. She developed the patent-pending Wired BioHealth™ Complete Panel, and she sees patients through her Atlanta and Alpharetta practices, where she uses personalized testing and plans to find root-cause imbalances.

It's all of the living that we do in-between that brings us to either end of the spectrum.

Her work is grounded in her "Prescription for Life" and "Four Pillars of Health" philosophy, an integrative model that brings together physical, emotional, intellectual and spiritual health. She has authored CE-accredited curricula for chiropractors and regularly trains practitioners in the clinical use of nutrigenomic and biochemical data.

On stage and on air

A trusted voice on behavioral health.

Dr. Higgins has spoken at TEDx, the Addiction Studies Institute at Ohio State Medical Center and the International Society of Substance Use Professionals. She has served as an advisor to the U.S. Surgeon General, hosted the Gracie Award-winning Dr Evelyn Higgins Show, and appeared on NBC Nightly News, Fox News and Newsmax TV. She is a member of the PharmacoGene Variation Consortium (PharmVar), a global think tank focused on pharmacogenomics and gene-based medicine.
