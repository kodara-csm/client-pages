My take

Most owners think the problem is lead gen, or hiring, or cash flow. It's almost never where they think.

If you run a brokerage or a small team and you're putting in 10, 12, sometimes 13 hour days with weekends on top, and the whole thing wobbles the second you step away, I want you to hear this. You're not lazy and you're not bad at business. You have a bottleneck problem, and the bottleneck is almost never the thing you're blaming.

I've sat inside more than 50 businesses, mortgage brokerages first, and the pattern is almost eerie. Two or three choke points cause the majority of the chaos. The owner is busy treating symptoms while the real cost sits somewhere they've never thought to look.

Why I can say that with a straight face

I built my own brokerage on hustle and instinct for years, and for a long stretch I was the business. Every deal, every decision, every fire landed on my desk. Then I went through my operations piece by piece and asked one question of every task: automate it, delegate it, or eliminate it. I did that across the whole company and went from 13 hour days with most weekends gone to 3 to 4 hours a day and no weekends, while the business kept growing.

Good operations are good operations. Whether you're a solo broker or running a team of 20, if the business can't function without you, the fix is shockingly similar.

I built my brokerage from the ground up, so I know this business from the inside, not from a slide deck. The bottlenecks don't care how big the team is. Find them, fix them, and everything else starts to move.

What I actually do

I built an AI-powered diagnostic that shows you exactly where your business is bleeding time and money, ranked by what each leak is costing you, in about 5 minutes, and it's free. You can take that and run with it yourself, or you can bring me in to fix it with you. Either way the work is the same. We find the two or three bottlenecks that actually matter, and we start there instead of trying to fix everything at once.
