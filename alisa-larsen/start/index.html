Questions

Before you begin

Can an AI really coach my voice?

Alisa AI is shaped entirely by Alisa's own teaching and her Fall in Love with Your Voice Framework, and it's built to keep everything gentle and one step at a time. It's a practice coach, not a medical or vocal-health service. If anything ever hurts, stop and check in with a professional.

Am I too old to start?

No. Many of the singers Alisa works with begin in their 50s, 60s and 70s, and that's a huge plus: more life experience makes you a more mature artist with more to say. There is no window that closed.

I always buy things and never use them. Will this be different?

That's exactly what Today's 15 Minutes is for. You never have to decide what to do, you just show up and say how much time you have. Premium goes further: the plan is proactive, so it reaches out to you instead of waiting for you to remember.

Will I have to perform or record myself?

Never. Everything starts privately, and performing is optional and on your timeline. The Permission Room is entirely yours.

What's the difference between Basic and Premium?

Basic is the coach you come to: daily sessions, the Permission Room, and answers to anything you ask. Premium is the coach that comes to you: it builds you a personal plan, checks in, tracks your progress, preps the songs you love, and unlocks Alisa's whole Brain.

Do I need Sing Without Thinking first?

No. It's a low-risk first taste for anyone who wants to start small, but you can subscribe to Basic or Premium directly.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, in two clicks. And your first month is covered by the Fall-In-Love-Or-It's-Free guarantee.
